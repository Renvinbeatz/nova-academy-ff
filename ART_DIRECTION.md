## Prompt compartilhado

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square course thumbnail icon, 1:1 image. Create a premium macOS-inspired 3D app icon illustration for a professional Free Fire video editing academy called NOVA. Subject: [subject below] Style: polished restrained 3D studio-rendered object, soft beveled edges, matte ivory and brushed champagne-gold surfaces, delicate realistic shadows, clean purposeful silhouette. Composition: one large centrally composed icon object, generous 17 percent padding, straight-on slight elevated perspective; designed to remain immediately legible at 56 pixels. Backdrop: uniform dark midnight navy #17202d filling the entire square, very subtle studio illumination. No text, no numbers, no words, no logos, no characters, no borders, no busy ornament. One single square icon image, not a sheet.
